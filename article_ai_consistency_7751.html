Decoding the AI Video Workflow: Stop the Slot Machine Waste

A common friction point is the "hidden cost" of AI generation. It is often "tricky" to know exactly how much it costs to get a professional result. Most amateur creators fall into the Slot Machine Trap: they type a prompt, hit generate, and hope for a masterpiece. When the AI fails, they spend more credits, until their budget is depleted with nothing but glitchy clips to show for it. This cycle happens because pure text-to-video gives zero control over the composition. The first fix to stop wasting money is using a Start Frame. Without an anchor image, the AI has to "invent" everything simultaneously. By selecting a production-ready start frame first, you ground the AI in a specific reality. However, using a bad quality start frame—one with blurry textures or "AI-looking" plasticky skin—will ruin the video. Professional workflows prioritize the creation of a "Visual Bible" before a single credit is spent on animation. Move from vague prompts to Intent-Based Prompting: define the 5 Pillars of every shot: Who, Where, What action, Camera Movement, and Mood. When you provide this structure, models can follow instructions with precision instead of "guessing" and failing.
