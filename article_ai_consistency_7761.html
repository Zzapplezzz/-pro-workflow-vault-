The 80% Failure Rate: Why Your AI Videos 'Cock Up' Big Time

Many creators are facing a hidden epidemic: the 80% failure rate in AI video generation. This is the result of the "Slot Machine Trap". When you rely on pure text-to-video prompts, you give the AI zero control over the composition, forcing it to "invent" everything simultaneously. The foundation of success is the Start Frame. As Adil explains, your start frame is responsible for 80% of your result. Without one, the AI has to dream up every pixel, leading to the "morphing" effect. Using a "bad quality" start frame—with blurry textures or "AI-looking" plasticky skin—is dangerous because the video carries those visual flaws through every second. Cinematic mastery requires Intent-Based Prompting. Define the 5 Pillars of your shot: Who, Where, What action, Camera Movement, and Mood. For specific details like emblems, you must move from being a prompt engineer to a movie director.
