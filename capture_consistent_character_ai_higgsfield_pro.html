The "Slot Machine" Trap

Most creators approach AI video like a casino. They type a long prompt into a tool like InVideo or Sora, hit generate, and pray. This is Text-to-Video in its most basic, uncontrolled form. The AI is forced to invent the subject, the motion, and the background simultaneously, leading to what we call "AI Chaos."

The biggest issue isn't the credits—it's the Face Drift. Between two shots, your character's eye color changes, or a birthmark moves from the forehead to the chin. To the human brain, this instantly feels "cheap" because we are biologically wired to notice these small inconsistencies.
