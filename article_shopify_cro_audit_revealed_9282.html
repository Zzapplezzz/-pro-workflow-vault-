What a "Revenue-First" Audit Analyzes

A professional-grade audit doesn't just skim your homepage. It systematically breaks down your entire conversion funnel through the lens of user experience (UX) and sales psychology. Key focus areas include:

  • Trust & Persuasion Signals: Analyzing if your "social proof" is actually persuasive.
  • Navigation Architecture: Ensuring the user journey is frictionless and intuitive.
  • Offer Psychology: Auditing how products are bundled to maximize AOV.
  • Behavioral Friction: Identifying where users lose "buying momentum."
